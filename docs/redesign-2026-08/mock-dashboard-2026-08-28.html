Round 3 — green as the main colour, not the sage

Old pine#14503A · dark, low chroma, yellow-leaning → Verbatim green#0E8A5F · mid-light, higher chroma, blue-leaning
  • All white. Canvas and tiles are the same white; tiles and the sidebar carry a soft shadow, so the background reads as “behind” because things sit on it, not because it is a different colour.
  • Green does four jobs, and only those: “you” in every chart, the primary button, the active-nav mark, and “good” (positive sentiment, upward deltas, the evidence chip). Everything else in the chrome stays grey, so the green reads as identity instead of a wash.
  • The shift away from the AI green is in lightness and chroma, not just hue: the old pine sat at ~L30 with muted saturation (the cluster-1 signature); this one is ~L50 and clean, closer to what Shopify and Mercury’s success colour do, and it stays legible as a 1.5 px underline and a 6 px bar.
  • Kept from round 2: orange competitor, grey category, amber mixed, red negative; claims still click open the voices, now marked only by a quiet dotted underline (no colour fill); no black blocks; serif for speech, mono for counts.
  • One consequence to accept: “you” and “positive” now share the green. They never meet in the same chart on this page, and “good for you” is the honest reading of both; if a page needs them apart (Voice’s movers), positive can drop to a lighter tint.
